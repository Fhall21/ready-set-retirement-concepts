HOW YOU WORK WITH INDIVIDUALS AND COUPLES
==========================================

12. Do you work with couples at all stages of your four-stage method - from the initial chat through to the final session?

13. What's your standard package? How many sessions, how long are they, do you provide a written plan, how often do you check in, and what are your fees?

14. For the first chat, do you offer phone, video, or both? And for follow-up sessions, do you work in person around Cairns, or by video, or a mix of both?

15. In the Services page, we mention diary entries like "Handover with Jo" and "the Esplanade" - are these real examples you'd be comfortable with people seeing, or are they just for illustration?

16. When you refer someone to counselling or other support, what's the exact wording you use? (The site currently says "I'll point you to the right support" - is that your language?)

17. On the Individual Services page, we mention a letter or document you provide. Are you happy with how it's described and willing to sign the version we've written?


WORKING WITH ORGANISATIONS
============================

18. Do you have any organisations or clients you'd be willing to name as case studies or testimonials? If so, would you be happy if we approached them for a quote?

19. For the "About the practitioner" section on the Organisation page, are these credentials right to lead for that audience: "MBA (HR)" and "Mental Health First Aider (Workplace)"?

20. Do you have a workshop testimonial or quote from a past client that you have permission to use on the site?

21. The Organisation page says people "tend to" struggle with retirement transitions - do you have your own data, research, or a published source you'd like to cite instead?

22. Would you be interested in linking the retirement wellbeing framing to workplace psychosocial hazards and duty of care? (For example, Queensland's Psychosocial Hazards Code of Practice?) This is optional - only if you want that angle.

23. What are the actual programme options? We have placeholders for session lengths, group sizes, and delivery methods (in-person, online, hybrid) - what do you actually offer?

24. What are the names and sequence of the three main steps or phases in your organisation programme? (Currently we have placeholders for things like timing and content.)

25. When you run a workshop or programme for an organisation, do you provide a de-identified summary or report back to them afterwards?

26. Do you have a standard email template you send to organisations when confirming a workshop or programme? If so, would you like us to use it instead of the placeholder we've created?
