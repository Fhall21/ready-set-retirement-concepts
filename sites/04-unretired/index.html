UNRETIRED

Forty years of planning for the money. About ten minutes planning for the person who has to live on it.

Ready Set Retirement is psychosocial retirement education, not financial advice. It is the yin to financial planning’s yang, and it is the half almost nobody is taught. Dr Sandra Walden-Pearson works with people three to ten years out from finishing, and with the ones already in it who found it sour.

human-centred retirement planning™

Retirement costs you your direction identity purpose status people direction, identity, purpose, status, people.

Superannuation covers none of the five. It was never designed to. A balance funds a lifestyle; it cannot buy a quality of life, and it has nothing to say about Monday.

The dark side

The farewell cake gets eaten on a Friday. On Monday there is no reason to be anywhere by eight. The people you saw five days a week were structural rather than chosen, and most of them do not ring. Someone asks what you do and the sentence you have used for thirty years no longer applies. This is the recognised dark side of retirement, and it arrives on schedule for people who did everything else right. It is not weakness. It is an unplanned transition, and unplanned transitions go badly whatever the balance says.

What education
actually does

Five things, in order. None of them are encouragement.

  1. 01
    One. Rehearsal

    You practise the next identity while you still have the old one to stand on. Three to ten years out is the working window. After the farewell it becomes repair work, and repair costs more.

  2. 02
    Two. Language

    Naming the four losses in advance makes them survivable. Most people reach their last week without ever being given the words, so when it lands they assume it is a private failure.

  3. 03
    Three. Structure

    Purpose, not hobbies. A week with real shape in it, designed before the last day of work rather than improvised in the third month, when the days have already started to run together.

  4. 04
    Four. People

    Rebuilding a social base that does not depend on a payroll system. Colleagues are proximity. Friendship is chosen. Very few people have counted how much of theirs was the former.

  5. 05
    Five. Evidence

    readysetretirement psychosocial planning® is a method with stages, delivered as training, workshops, seminars, one-to-one and group coaching, consulting and speaking. You can tell whether you are through a stage. That is the difference between education and reassurance.
