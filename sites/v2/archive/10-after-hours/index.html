The cost is already on your books. It is filed under something else.

Australians are retiring at the fastest rate in the nation's history. For an employer that is not a demographic curiosity. It is a queue, and the people at the front of it hold the parts of the business that were never written down.

Pipeline

Retirement is the only departure you can see coming five years out, and it is the one most organisations plan for last. Succession gets raised at the exit interview. By then the successor has had no apprenticeship and the leaver has no reason to give one.

Mastery

What walks out is the undocumented kind of knowledge. Which supplier will actually answer at six on a Friday. Why that clause is in the contract. Which failure the process was written to prevent. None of it survives a handover template, because none of it was ever in one.

Cost

Recruitment and ramp-up are the invoice you can see. The quieter charges are the productivity dip in the team left behind, the risk that quietly returns once the person who remembered it has gone, and what the rest of your workforce concludes when thirty years ends with a cake and a car park.

A person who is unprepared for retirement does not leave cleanly. They disengage months before the date, and the handover goes with them.
