What is out there

Four things leave on the same afternoon.

All four were attached to the job. None of them are money problems, which is why the money plan never reached them.

Direction

Forty years of somewhere to be, finished on a Friday.

Identity

Introduced by your job title for thirty years. Then not.

Purpose

Work answered what you are for, daily. The question outlives it.

Connection

Most work friendships were structural. Remove the structure and you find out.

Two ways in

Same ground, two starting points.

If it is your date

Three to ten years out is the cheap end. You still have time, colleagues, energy and a title that opens doors.

One-to-one and small-group work on the four things above. Already retired and finding it sour counts too.

If it is your workforce

Australians are retiring faster than at any point in the nation's history, and the first to go are the people who know how the place actually works.

Training, workshops and advisory work that treat a departure as workforce planning rather than a cake.
