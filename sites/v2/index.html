Hi San — here they are again, with the lights on.

Thank you for the notes. They were unusually useful, because they said the same thing seven times: the writing landed, and the colour did not. Six of the seven got marked down on colour alone — dark, dreary, sombre, monochrome, depressing. So this round is a colour round. Nothing that was working has been moved.

Two of them have also been rebuilt. Vitals, because you singled out the four dials and the covered/not-covered table, so those are now the whole page and the rest is gone. The Map, because you said it was genius but took too much thinking — so the surveyor's apparatus is gone and one clear image is left.

The one thing we need from you

A — violet A — coral B — orchid B — azure

Every page now carries a small A / B switch in the bottom-right corner. It changes the colour and nothing else, so a preference you express is a preference about colour and not about layout. Your choice follows you from page to page, so you can walk the whole set in one and then walk it again in the other.

A is violet and coral — warmer, more energetic, more distinctive. B is orchid and azure — cooler, more corporate, better suited to an HR or board audience. Both are brighter than anything you saw last time. Tell us which one you keep reaching for.
