READY SET RETIREMENT

A film about the part nobody rehearses.

Presented in three acts

Psychosocial retirement education with Dr Sandra Walden-Pearson. Far North Queensland.

I

Act one. THE PLAN

Forty years of contributions. Statements read. Projections revised. A date circled on a calendar you will not need.

Australians are retiring at the fastest rate in the nation’s history. Nearly all of them have a number. Very few have a plan for the person.

Money answers one question, and answers it well. It funds a lifestyle. It cannot buy a quality of life.

II

Act two. THE DROP

DIRECTION

IDENTITY

PURPOSE

CONNECTION

All four go in the same season. The farewell cake is still in the fridge.

This is the recognised dark side of retirement. It is common, it is well documented, and it is almost never planned for.

III

Act three. THE WORK

None of this is a character flaw. It is a planning gap, and planning gaps close with preparation.

Ready Set Retirement is the yin to financial planning’s yang. Not advice about the money. Preparation for the person who stops earning it.
