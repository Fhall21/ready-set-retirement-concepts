01
VitalsRebuilt
View v2 → View v1 ◫ Compare Side-by-Side
Concept
Clinical instrument panel with interactive diagnostic risk gauges.
What changed in v2
Two banks of dials became one bank with an unprepared / prepared switch. The ECG trace is gone. Diagnostic table cut from 11 rows to 7. Two thirds of words removed to eliminate cognitive overwhelm.
02
The MapRebuilt
View v2 → View v1 ◫ Compare Side-by-Side
Concept
Survey cartography route traversal as terrain moves beneath.
What changed in v2
One route, three kinds of ground, five stops. Grid references, elevation, bearings, scale bar, north arrow and 10-item legend removed along with technical jargon. Annotations simplified from 12 to 4.
03
The Long LightIterated
View v2 → View v1 ◫ Compare Side-by-Side
Concept
A24 documentary, warm 35mm film strip letterboxed in cinema panel.
What changed in v2
Page-wide vignette and heavy grain removed. Frames re-graded warm and bright. Film now opens directly on her face rather than a figure at the end of a jetty.
04
Second HorizonIterated
View v2 → View v1 ◫ Compare Side-by-Side
Concept
Scrubbed celestial arc over a fixed horizon.
What changed in v2
Night sky previously felt depressing ("getting darker as years progress"). Now runs pre-dawn to full morning, inverting to dark ink on light once the sun rises. "Sunset years" headline removed.
05
UnretiredIterated
View v2 → View v1 ◫ Compare Side-by-Side
Concept
Swiss poster, kinetic type with hard polarity inversion.
What changed in v2
No longer pure monochrome; integrated violet and coral brand scheme. Poster type sized down by one-third so it feels emphatic rather than shouting.
06
The Quiet RoomIterated
View v2 → View v1 ◫ Compare Side-by-Side
Concept
Tactile paper, a single thread drawing itself down the page.
What changed in v2
Aged cream and paper-fibre texture removed for crisp white ground. Photographs opened up. Thread is now bright violet into coral.
07
Third ActIterated
View v2 → View v1 ◫ Compare Side-by-Side
Concept
70mm title cards, cinematic aperture opening and closing.
What changed in v2
Black, white and red balance refined. Red is brighter, dark areas carry violet cast, and Act III (the act about the work) inverts to a bright ground.

On Cognitive Load
