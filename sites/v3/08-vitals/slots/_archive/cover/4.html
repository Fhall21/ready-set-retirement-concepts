A money plan is excellent at the things it covers.

Not one line in the lower half is a money problem. That is why the money plan was never going to reach it.

  • Income, tax and drawdown — covered by the money plan.
  • Super, pension and health cover — covered by the money plan.
  • The house, and whether to move — covered by the money plan.
  • What a Tuesday looks like — not covered by the money plan.
  • Who you are without the title — not covered by the money plan.
  • Something to be useful for — not covered by the money plan.
  • People who still call — not covered by the money plan.

One funds the lifestyle. The other decides whether there is a life in it.
