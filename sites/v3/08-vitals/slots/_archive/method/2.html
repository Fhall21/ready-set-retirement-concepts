Forty years of planning for a date. About ten minutes planning for a life.

Education is what moves it.

Ready Set Retirement is psychosocial retirement education. It is not financial advice.

It is the yin to financial planning's yang, and it decides whether the money you saved buys a life you recognise.

The work starts three to ten years out, while you still have leverage: time, colleagues, energy, and a title that still opens doors.

Preparation is cheap at that stage. Afterwards it becomes repair, and repair costs more.

readysetretirement psychosocial planning® is a structured program — that is human-centred retirement planning™, and it is teachable.

What that looks like in practice

Workshops Half a day or a series, run in-house or open to the public.
Coaching One-to-one or group, for the person who cannot say this out loud at work yet.
Consulting For organisations putting retirement into workforce planning rather than payroll.
