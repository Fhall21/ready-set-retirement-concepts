This is the recognised dark side of retirement, and it arrives on schedule for the people who did everything else right. It is not a weakness. It is an unplanned transition, and unplanned transitions go badly whatever the balance says.

Five things education actually does.

Rehearsal — practise the next identity while you still have the old one.
Language — name the four losses before they arrive unannounced.
Structure — a week with real shape, designed rather than improvised.
People — a social base that does not depend on a payroll system.
Evidence — a method with stages, so you can tell whether you are through one.
