What nobody puts in the speech

We spend forty years planning for retirement financially and roughly ten minutes planning who we will be when it arrives.

The money question gets a professional, a strategy, an annual review and a spreadsheet that runs to age ninety. The other question gets a good luck and a card.

Money funds a lifestyle. It cannot buy a quality of life, and it has never once told anyone what to do with a Wednesday.

Loss of direction, identity, purpose and connection. Recognised in the research on retirement. Rarely mentioned at the farewell.

One half of this problem has an entire industry attached to it. The other has almost nobody.

This is the question psychosocial retirement education actually answers.
