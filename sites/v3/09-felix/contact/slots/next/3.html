What happens after you book

Nothing to prepare, and nothing to sign up for.

  1. You pick a time

    Or send a message and Dr San will find one with you.

  2. You have a chat

    Thirty minutes with Dr San, by video call or phone. Talk about whatever’s on your mind.

  3. You decide

    If it would help to keep going, she’ll suggest what that could look like. If not, that’s fine too.

Good to know

Can my partner join?

Yes, you’re welcome to chat together.

Is it too early if I’m years away?

No. Dr San works with people three to ten years from retirement.

Is this financial advice?

No. It sits alongside your financial plan, looking after the personal and social side of retiring.
