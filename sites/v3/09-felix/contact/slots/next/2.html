What happens after you book

Nothing to prepare, and nothing to sign up for.

Is the chat really free?

Yes. It’s 30 minutes, and there’s nothing to pay.

Do I need to prepare anything?

No. If something about retiring has been on your mind, bring that. If not, that’s fine too.

Can my partner join?

Yes, you’re welcome to chat together.

I’m not retiring for years. Is it too early?

No. Dr San works with people three to ten years from retirement.

Is this financial advice?

No. Dr San helps with the personal and social side of retiring. Keep working with your financial adviser on the money; this sits alongside it.

I’m asking for an organisation. Where do I start?

Send a message and choose “An organisation”, or read about workshops for organisations first.
