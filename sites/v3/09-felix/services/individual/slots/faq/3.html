Things people often ask.

Is this financial advice?

No. Dr San isn’t a financial adviser. This sits alongside the plan you already have: one pays for the life, the other helps you live it.

Is it counselling?

No. It’s education and planning: practical work on your weeks, your people and what gets you up in the morning. If something bigger comes up, Dr San can point you to the right support.

When should I start?

Three to ten years out is the easiest time, while you still have colleagues, routine and energy to draw on. But there’s no wrong time for a first chat.

Can my partner come too?

Yes, you’re welcome to come together. Two people retiring into one house is well worth planning for.

Do I need to live in Far North Queensland?

No. Sessions can run by video wherever you are, and in person around Cairns.

What does it cost?

The first 30-minute chat is free. Dr San will talk you through the rest then, with no pressure either way.
