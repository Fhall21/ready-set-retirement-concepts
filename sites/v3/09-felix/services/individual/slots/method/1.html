So what do we actually do together?

It’s a structured program in four stages. You’ll always know which one you’re in.

Your retirement plan 4 stages · on your own or as a couple
StageWhat we doWhat you leave with
1Map We look at what work is quietly doing for you: your routine, your sense of who you are, your purpose, your people. A clear picture of what leaves with the job.
2Test We try out an ordinary week without work, on paper and then for real, and notice what goes missing. The gaps, named, before you meet them.
3Build We plan what will fill those gaps. Real things, close to home, with people you choose. A plan for your weeks, in plain words.
4Practise You try the new pieces while you still have somewhere to be on Monday, and we adjust as we go. Habits that already work on the day you finish.

Bring your partner if you like. Retirement changes the whole household, not just one diary.
