So what do we actually do together?

It’s a structured program in four stages. You’ll always know which one you’re in.

  1. 1

    Map

    We look at what work is quietly doing for you: your routine, your sense of who you are, your purpose, your people.

    You leave withA clear picture of what leaves with the job.

  2. 2

    Test

    We try out an ordinary week without work, on paper and then for real, and notice what goes missing.

    You leave withThe gaps, named, before you meet them.

  3. 3

    Build

    We plan what will fill those gaps. Real things, close to home, with people you choose.

    You leave withA plan for your weeks, in plain words.

  4. 4

    Practise

    You try the new pieces while you still have somewhere to be on Monday, and we adjust as we go.

    You leave withHabits that already work on the day you finish.

Bring your partner if you like. Retirement changes the whole household, not just one diary.
