What Dr San runs

Each one stands on its own. Choose what suits your people, and each is shaped around the work they do.

  • Preparing for retirement

    The core workshop. People look at the parts of retirement money doesn’t cover, and leave with a first plan of their own.

    Who
    Staff three to ten years from retiring
    Length
    Half or full day · up to 20 people

  • Lunchtime seminar

    A friendly introduction to the human side of retirement. A gentle way to see who’s interested.

    Who
    Anyone, at any stage
    Length
    60 minutes · any size

  • Talks and keynotes

    Dr San speaking on why a good retirement needs more than a financial plan.

    Where
    Conferences, leadership days, member events
    Length
    20–45 minutes

  • Training for managers

    How to talk with someone about retiring, kindly and without assumptions.

    Who
    People leaders and HR
    Length
    Half day · up to 15 people

  • One-to-one sessions

    Private follow-up for staff who want to go further after a workshop.

    Who
    Individual staff, or a couple
    Length
    60 minutes
