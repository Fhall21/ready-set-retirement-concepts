Preparing for retirement

A workshop for staff three to ten years from retiring

People look at the parts of retirement money doesn’t cover: what their weeks will hold, who they’ll see, and what they’ll say when someone asks “so what do you do now?”

They leave with a first plan of their own, written in their own words.
