What a morning with Dr San looks like

The half-day workshop, as it usually runs. Times move to suit your roster.

  1. Before

    A 30-minute call with you about your people, your workplace and anything to handle gently.

    You let staff know it’s on. Dr San can give you a short note to send.

  2. On the day

    • 9:00Welcome
    • 9:20The first Monday
    • 10:00What leaves with the job
    • 10:45Morning tea
    • 11:05An ordinary week
    • 11:50Talking about it at home
    • 12:15First steps

    You provide a room with tables, a screen and morning tea.

  3. After

    Everyone keeps their workbook. Anyone who wants to go further can book a private session.

    You get a short summary of the themes that came up, with nothing that identifies anyone.
