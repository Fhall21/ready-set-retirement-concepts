When someone retires, the paperwork gets done. The rest is left to them.

Routine, purpose and daily company all leave with the job, on the same day.

Why it matters to your organisation:

  1. Retirement becomes something people can talk about

    Staff close to the end often go quiet about their plans. A workshop makes it an ordinary, open conversation, so timing and handover can be planned together.

  2. The last year is spent handing over, not counting down

    People who can picture what comes next tend to use their final months well. What they know has a better chance of staying with you.

  3. The people who stay notice

    How an organisation sees off its long-serving people tells everyone else how they’ll be looked after too.

It’s easier to prepare while they’re still at work, with colleagues around them.
