How I came to this work

I started out teaching English. Language was my first love. I even went back for an honours year in linguistics.

Then came decades of leading people. In schools, community organisations and business, the work kept coming back to people: how they grow, and how they cope when things change.

Along the way, a PhD. An MBA in HR, then a PhD in Business. People say I’m a people person, a businessperson and a nerd. Fair enough.

And a lot of farewells. I watched capable people leave work with the money sorted and nothing planned for who they’d be next. Nobody had shown them how.
