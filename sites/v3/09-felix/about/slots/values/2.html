What sitting down with me is like

What I will do

  • Plain talk. I’ll tell you plainly what your plans cover, and what they don’t.
  • A conversation, not an assessment. You talk, I listen, and we work it out together.
  • Alongside your adviser. I’m not a financial adviser. My work sits next to theirs.
  • No rush. Ten years out or three, we start from where you are.

What I won’t do

  • Sell you a product.
  • Give you advice about money.
  • Hurry you towards a decision.
  • Treat you like a patient.
